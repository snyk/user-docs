# Invite users

Navigate to **Settings** > **Security and access** > **Members** and invite your team members, applying the role alignments decided in Phase 1 for each member.

{% hint style="info" %}
Snyk generally recommends inviting members after you import your Projects. You can bring in a few initial members to start and then do a wider rollout. You can do this at any time as part of stages four and five. Snyk strongly recommends communicating what is expected before the invitation, and then throughout the various stages as you turn on pull request checks, enable pipelines, and change policies and practices.
{% endhint %}
