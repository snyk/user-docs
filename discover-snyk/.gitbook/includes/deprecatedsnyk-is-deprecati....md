{% hint style="warning" %}
**Deprecated**

Snyk is deprecating the Snyk Learn platform. **After January 15, 2027, Snyk Learn is unavailable**, including all capabilities, integrations, and the Learning Management add-on.

* **Enterprise and add-on customers:** Download your data before this date. Visit [Snyk Learn reporting](../../developer-education-with-snyk-learn/snyk-learn/snyk-learn-reports/) and [Snyk Learn API](../../developer-education-with-snyk-learn/snyk-learn/snyk-learn-api.md) for instructions.
* **Individual users:** Download your Learning Path certificates before this date. Navigate to **Your learning** > [**Certificates of completion**](../../developer-education-with-snyk-learn/snyk-learn/your-learning/#certificates-of-completion) and follow the instructions.

If you have questions, contact your Snyk account team or submit a request at the [Snyk Support site](https://support.snyk.io/s/).
{% endhint %}
