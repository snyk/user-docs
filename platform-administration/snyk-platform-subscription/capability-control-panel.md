# Capability control panel

In the Tenant settings in the Snyk Web UI, select **Capability Access** under **Security and access** to open the capability control panel. The control panel is the menu for Tenant Admins to enable or disable Snyk capabilities. Click the **Enable** toggle for each capability you want to use. You can toggle a capability off at any time to stop consuming credits for capabilities entitled through credits.

All capabilities default to off for a new customer, so you cannot access most of the Snyk Platform until a Tenant Admin chooses which capabilities to enable. Configure this access as your first step when you onboard as a new customer on the Snyk Platform Subscription.

Settings at the Tenant level determine capability access for all associated Groups and Organizations. This off-by-default policy ensures that Snyk consumes credits only with Tenant Admin permission. Only Tenant Admins can adjust the control panel.

{% hint style="info" %}
If your contract has hybrid licensing, with active subscriptions for both credits and SKU-based licensing, the control panel can toggle only the capabilities that are not subscribed to on SKUs.
{% endhint %}

The **Your Plan** page under **Plan and billing** shows a summary of the capabilities included in your subscriptions, by licensing type. For details, see [Viewing your Plan](enterprise-contract-types/viewing-your-plan.md). Capabilities you can toggle in the control panel show Credits as their billing model. Any SKU-based subscriptions show their quantity and SKU-level licensing measurement instead.
