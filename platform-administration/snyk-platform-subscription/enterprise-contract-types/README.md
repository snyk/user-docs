# Enterprise contract types

Snyk offers two licensing structures for Enterprise plans. The legacy structure is SKU-based: each SKU licenses a specific capability, priced on the annual high-watermark volume of licenses you procure. You can contract SKUs independently or as bundles that appear as one order-form line item while covering several capabilities. For the usage settings that apply to this structure, visit [SKU-based licensing usage settings](../legacy-plans/usage-settings.md).

The current structure is the [Snyk Platform Subscription](../snyk-platform-subscription-mechanics.md), a consumption-based license. You pre-purchase a balance of credits valid for your full contract term and draw it down as you use capabilities across the platform.

To see which structure applies to each capability in your plan, visit [View your plan](../viewing-your-plan.md).
