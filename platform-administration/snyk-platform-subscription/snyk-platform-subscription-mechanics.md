# How the Snyk Platform Subscription works

The Snyk Platform Subscription is the default Snyk Enterprise plan. You purchase credits at the beginning of your subscription, valid for the full term of your contract. Credits carry entitlements for all capabilities across the platform and are fungible across capabilities. You consume credits from your balance based on your contracted rate card. Every order form that contains a subscription for credits includes a link to a rate card that describes how use of the platform translates into credit consumption. For details, see [Rate card and your contract](#rate-card-and-your-contract).

Snyk deducts credits from your balance daily, based on the volume of billable events each day. You can use credits at any pace over the duration of a contract to accommodate natural ramping, seasonality, or growth. Unused credits remaining when a contract ends are non-refundable and do not automatically carry over to a renewal. To follow your consumption, visit [Track credit consumption](understanding-credit-consumption.md).

If you exhaust your initial balance of credits, you can continue to use the platform on an on-demand basis. Snyk invoices on-demand consumption at the per-credit price in your contract.

When Snyk introduces new platform capabilities as Generally Available, Snyk automatically adds them to your rate card. As a result, you gain immediate access to new platform functionality, and the credit-consumption mechanics stay transparent and documented. New capabilities always default to off in the [capability control panel](capability-control-panel.md), so a Tenant Admin must authorize them before they consume credits.

## Rate card and your contract

Your rate card is the one linked in your order form. Every Enterprise order form that includes credits links a specific, versioned rate card, and that version governs your contract for its full term. Refer to it for the consumption rates and units of measure that apply to you.

Snyk publishes the current rate card at [Credit-based billing policies](https://snyk.io/policies/credit-based-billing/). That page always shows the latest version, which can differ from the version linked in your order form.
